<h1 align="center">Mixify</h1>

<p align="center">
  A fast, good-looking and highly customisable music player for Windows.<br>
  Play your own music files, build playlists, restyle the whole app and keep track of your listening.
</p>

---

## Download

Get the latest version from the **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** page and download `Mixify.exe` (or the `.zip`).

Mixify is a single app: no installer, just run it.

**Requirements**
- Windows 10 (version 1809) or newer, 64-bit
- Your own music files: mp3, wav, flac, m4a, aac, wma

## Getting started

1. Open Mixify and type a username. Optionally add a profile picture.
2. Pick your music folder. Every subfolder becomes a playlist.
3. Press play. Create your own playlists with **+**, drag songs in from Explorer, or right-click a song to add it to a playlist.

## Features

**Player**
- Playlists from your folders, plus your own playlists with custom names and pictures
- Queue with drag-and-drop, shuffle, loop, and sort by name, author or your own order
- Crossfade between songs, a 6-band equalizer and Auto EQ, stereo/mono output
- Full-screen "Now playing" view and a taskbar mini player
- Media keys, Bluetooth headset buttons and the Windows media pop-up all work
- Visualizer in the title bar with many looks, colours and gradients
- Waveform seek bar with your own colours
- Search bar (Ctrl+F) for songs and playlists

**Make it yours**
- Themes: Windows XP, Windows 95, Winamp, Aero Glass, Synthwave, Terminal, Paper Light and more, or make your own
- Custom fonts, text colours and background pictures or GIFs with blur
- Playlist icon editor with gradients and symbols
- Tags with emoji, custom song names, artists and pictures (you can copy a picture from another song)
- Rearrangeable Home screen, library grid or list, pinned and hidden playlists
- Custom keyboard shortcuts

**Your listening**
- Profile page with hours listened, plays, top 10 songs and your day streak
- **Achievements** with progress bars, popups and sound
- **Miximonth**: a monthly recap in the style of Spotify Wrapped

**Extras**
- Download songs from YouTube as mp3 (see the note below)
- Low Performance Mode for older computers
- Runs in the tray and can start with Windows
- Settings backup and import

## Updates

New versions are published here on the Releases page. Download the new `Mixify.exe` and replace the old one. Your settings, playlists and statistics are kept in `%AppData%\Mixify`, so nothing is lost.

## YouTube downloads

Mixify can convert YouTube links to mp3. The first time you use it, it offers to fetch two free helper tools (yt-dlp and ffmpeg) into `%AppData%\Mixify\tools`.

**Only download music you have the right to download.** You are responsible for following the laws and the terms of the services you use.

## Privacy

Mixify works offline and keeps everything on your computer. It doesn't collect data or send your listening history anywhere.

## About this project

Mixify was "vibe coded": it was built with the help of an AI assistant (Claude by Anthropic), with me deciding what it should do and testing the result. It works well for me, but it hasn't had the review a professionally written app would get, so expect the occasional bug and please report it.
